The NHS App should be accessible to everyone. This includes people with disabilities and people who use assistive technology, such as screen readers.

As public sector bodies, we must meet the [Public Sector Bodies (Websites and Mobile Applications) Accessibility Regulations 2018](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/952/contents/made). This applies to all of us in NHS organisations who build websites or mobile apps.

## The NHS App

The NHS App team is responsible for the pages and features built as part of the app itself. These are covered by the [NHS App accessibility statement](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-app/about/privacy-legal-information/nhs-app-accessibility-statement/).

Read about accessibility in:

- [the web app](/accessibility/web/)
- [the iOS app](/accessibility/ios/)
- [the Android app](/accessibility/android/)

## Integrated services

Many services in the NHS App are built by other teams. These are not covered by the NHS App accessibility statement. Each integrating service team is responsible for its own accessibility compliance and accessibility statement.

Read about [accessibility for integrated services](/accessibility/integrated-services/), including how to get an audit, publish an accessibility statement and share it with us.
